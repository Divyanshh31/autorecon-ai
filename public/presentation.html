THE MERCHANT FINANCIAL CRISIS

Problem Statement: Invoice & Payment Tracker Challenge

Official Challenge

Build an invoice management system with customer creation, line items, status updates, search/filtering, overdue identification, summary cards, and printable views.

Gateway MDR Leakage

Payment gateways overcharge transaction fees (e.g. charging corporate 3.25% rates on zero-cap RuPay/UPI orders).

Section 43B(h) MSME Tax Penalties

Unpaid MSME vendor invoices exceeding 45 days are disallowed as business expenses, causing tax penalties.

Payroll TDS Delays

Managing worker payouts alongside 10% Section 192 TDS requires manual compliance calculation.
