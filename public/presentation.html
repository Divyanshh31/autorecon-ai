THE MERCHANT FINANCIAL CRISIS

Problem Statement: Invoice & Payment Tracker Challenge

Official Challenge Requirement

Build an invoice management system with customer creation, multiple line items, payment status updates, search/filtering, overdue identification, summary cards, and printable views.

Gateway Fee Overcharges: Gateways overcharge transaction MDR fees (e.g. charging corporate 3.25% rates on zero-cap RuPay/UPI orders).
Section 43B(h) MSME Tax Penalties: Unpaid MSME vendor invoices past 45 days are disallowed as business expenses, causing tax penalties.
Payroll TDS Delays: Managing worker payouts alongside 10% Section 192 TDS requires manual compliance calculation.
