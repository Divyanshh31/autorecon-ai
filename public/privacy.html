Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 16, 2026 : Version 2.4

1. Executive Overview & Data Controller Scope

AutoRecon AI operates as an enterprise payment gateway reconciliation, ERP ledger bridge, and financial operations engine. This Privacy Policy details how we handle merchant order records, payment gateway telemetry, nodal bank settlement statements, and payroll dataset inputs processed through our software platform.

2. Data Collected & Processing Mandates

To execute 3-way reconciliation, merchant MDR fee mismatch detection, and TallyPrime XML voucher formatting, AutoRecon AI processes:

  • Gateway Telemetry: Transaction IDs, order reference numbers, payment channel methods (UPI, Netbanking, Cards), MDR fee deductions, GST tax components, and settlement timestamp data.
  • Nodal Settlement Data: Bank UTR numbers, credit timestamps, and gross payout amounts.
  • Accounts Payable & Payroll Inputs: Vendor invoice numbers, GSTIN identifiers, MSME status classifications, employee CTC structures, Section 192 TDS withholdings, and EPF values provided explicitly by the enterprise user.

3. Security, Encryption & Workspace Isolation

We enforce rigorous cybersecurity controls across all infrastructure tiers:

  • Data in Transit: All API requests, webhook ingestions, and browser communications are encrypted using TLS 1.3 protocols.
  • Data at Rest: Merchant datasets and encrypted auth tokens are stored using AES-256 encryption.
  • Tenant Isolation: Each enterprise workspace maintains complete logical data partition isolation. Merchant financial data is strictly accessible only to authorized credentials of that specific workspace.

4. Non-Disclosure & Third-Party Monetization Ban

AutoRecon AI strictly adheres to a zero-monetization policy regarding user financial records. We do not sell, license, rent, or trade merchant transaction logs, fee structures, customer rosters, or payroll registers to any third-party advertising, analytics, or data aggregation brokers.

5. Compliance with DPDP Act 2023 & RBI Directives

Our data handling practices comply with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 of India and Reserve Bank of India (RBI) payment aggregator data security frameworks. Webhook validation headers undergo HMAC-SHA256 signature verification to ensure message authenticity and prevent replay tampering.

6. Data Retention & Erasure Rights

Enterprise users retain complete ownership over their financial ledgers. You may export processed reconciliation reports, TallyPrime XML vouchers, or request complete purge of historical upload batches at any time through account settings.

7. Contact Information

For inquiries regarding data protection protocols or compliance audits, contact our Data Protection Desk at dpo@autorecon.ai.
